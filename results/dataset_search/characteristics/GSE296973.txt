!Series_overall_design = Peripheral blood samples from PE high-risk and control groups were collected in EDTA anticoagulant tubes, immediately mixed with TRIzol Reagent by vigorous vortexing to ensure complete cell lysis and RNA stabilization. Differential gene expression analysis focused on PE-associated pathways (angiogenesis, immune dysregulation) using DESeq2 with thresholds of |log2FC| ≥1 and adjusted p <0.05.
